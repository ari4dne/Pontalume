# Fine-tune your light's colors

Inexpensive RGB lights usually aren't color calibrated, and your room can change how a color looks. A light may seem too bright, or have an unwanted tint. You can fine-tune the colors in Pontalume for your lamp and your room.

Not connected yet? Start with the [quick start](quick-start.md).

## Steps

1. On the **Dashboard** tab, click the palette button at the top right of the color swatch. The **Adjust Broadcast Color** window opens. It adjusts the color for your current presence status.
2. Pick the color you want with the color wheel, or type exact **Red**, **Green** and **Blue** values, or a **Hex Code**. **Current** shows the color now, and **New** shows your change.
3. If your lamp supports brightness, move the brightness slider ([see the note below](#a-note-on-brightness)).
4. Click **Test on device** to see the change on the lamp. Adjust and test again until you like it.
5. Click **Update profile** to keep it. For a profile that comes with Pontalume, this saves your changes as a new copy. For a custom profile you made or imported, it updates that file.
6. Click **Cancel** to leave without saving. The lamp goes back to the color it had before.

To tune another status, such as In a call, set that status in Teams first, then repeat the steps.

## A note on brightness

Pontalume sends brightness as a number from 0 to 255. Some lamps expect a smaller range, often 0 to 100. Those lamps may ignore brightness commands or behave oddly when they get a larger number.

If your lamp does that, keep the slider at about 39% or lower (a value of 100 or less) and test again.
